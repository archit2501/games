# 5 Fun Offline Mobile Games

A collection of 5 simple, unique HTML5 games that work offline on any device with a browser. No installation required - just open and play!

---

## Games

### 1. Color Clash
**Type:** Arcade / Reflex

Colored orbs fall from the sky. Your paddle can change colors - catch orbs that match your paddle color for points, but avoid mismatched ones or lose a life!

**How to Play:**
- Tap **LEFT** side of screen → Move paddle left
- Tap **RIGHT** side of screen → Move paddle right
- Tap **CENTER** of screen → Cycle paddle color (Red → Teal → Yellow)
- Match colors = +10 points
- Wrong color = -1 life
- Speed increases as you score higher

---

### 2. Memory Rhythm
**Type:** Puzzle / Memory

A musical Simon-says game. Watch the colored tiles light up in sequence, then tap them back in the same order. Each round adds one more tile to remember!

**How to Play:**
- Watch the pattern of tiles lighting up
- Tap the tiles in the exact same order
- Tap in rhythm for bonus points
- Each correct sequence adds another tile
- One mistake and it's game over

---

### 3. Gravity Flip
**Type:** Puzzle / Platformer

Navigate a ball through obstacle-filled levels by flipping gravity. Your ball moves automatically - your only control is switching between falling up and falling down!

**How to Play:**
- Tap **ANYWHERE** → Flip gravity (up ↔ down)
- Collect ⭐ stars for bonus points
- Avoid red spikes
- Reach the EXIT to complete the level
- 10 levels with increasing difficulty

---

### 4. Orbit Defense
**Type:** Arcade / Strategy

You control a planet at the center of the screen. Asteroids approach from all directions - deploy orbital shields to deflect them and survive wave after wave!

**How to Play:**
- Tap **ANYWHERE** → Deploy a shield in that direction
- Shields orbit around your planet automatically
- Shields block and damage asteroids
- Shield energy regenerates over time
- Larger asteroids need multiple hits
- Survive waves to progress

---

### 5. Word Snake
**Type:** Puzzle / Word Game

Classic snake gameplay meets word puzzles. Guide your snake to collect letters and spell the target word. Collect letters in the correct order - wrong letters make your snake grow longer!

**How to Play:**
- **Swipe** or use **arrow buttons** to change direction
- Collect letters to spell the target word (shown at top)
- Letters must be collected in order
- Correct letter = points + progress
- Wrong letter = snake grows longer
- Complete words to reset snake length
- Categories include: Fruits, Animals, Colors, Foods, Nature

## Install as Android App (APK)

Each game folder contains PWA files ready for APK conversion:

### Quick Method (PWA Builder):
1. Host the game online (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel)
2. Go to [pwabuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com/)
3. Enter your game URL
4. Click "Package for stores" → "Android" → Download APK

### Detailed Instructions:
See [BUILD_APK.md](BUILD_APK.md) for complete step-by-step guide.

### Game Folders (PWA-ready):
```
games/
├── color-clash/      → Color Clash APK
├── memory-rhythm/    → Memory Rhythm APK
├── gravity-flip/     → Gravity Flip APK
├── orbit-defense/    → Orbit Defense APK
└── word-snake/       → Word Snake APK
```
